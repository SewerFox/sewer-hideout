Welcome to the 1st Edition of STK Enduro tournament!

This tournament will be played between October and November of 2026, starting October 3 and ending November 14.
It's being organized by Gusta, with some support by FabianF.
The races will happen in the server Konqi's Endurance Resort.
You don't need to register to participate in this event.
Just enter in the dedicated server on the scheduled days (see calendar below) and have fun :)
Also, please make sure to read the rules below.

1 Calendar

All rounds will start on a Saturday, at 16:00 UTC (converted times to your region in: Time zone converter).

Click in any scheduled race to watch its recording

1.1 Opening stage

All these races will be played in normal direction!

  • October 3: STK Enterprise 20 laps – Cornfield Crossing 20 laps
  • October 10: Lost Chasm 20 laps – Imminent Cold Effects 20 laps (both add-ons);
  • October 17: Northern Resort 20 laps – Supertux Raceway 20 laps (add-on).

1.2 Closing stage

All these races will be played in reverse direction!

  • October 31: Hacienda 20 laps – Shifting Sands 20 laps;
  • November 7: Tux Tollway 20 laps – Green Valley 20 laps (both add-ons);
  • November 14: Antediluvian Abyss 20 laps – Subsea 12 laps (add-on).

2.2 Scoring system

The tournament will use the following basic system (based on F1 2000s system):
10 – 8 – 6 – 5 – 4 – 3 – 2 – 1 point(s) for the first eight players each race.

3 Rules

3.1 Schedule

  • All rounds will start on a Saturday, at 16:00 UTC (converted times to your region in: Time zone converter).
  • The first race will start at most 5 minutes after the scheduled time (to wait for more players), and the second race will start after a break of 5–7 minutes, as it's exhausting to play two 20 laps races without resting a little :)
  • All races are planned to have 20 laps, so each race will likely last 20–30 minutes.

3.2 Add-on tracks

  • Some rounds will contain some add-on tracks. You MUST have these add-ons installed. If not, you won't be able to play on these tracks (you can just spectate in this case).
  • You can install these add-ons here. In the server motd, there will be a direct link to install the add-ons inside the game.

3.3 Prohibited behaviour list

  • Don't drive in the reverse direction of the players (or do anything that will disturb, like stopping in the middle of the track without any reason). If you try to do this, well… the server (that was modded by Heuchi1/kimden) will just kick you :)
  • Don't offend anyone with extremely heavy words (racism or another type of prejudice, many swear words directed at some players, the organizers or anyone else, or any big scuffle). If you dare to do this, you can be banned for the rest of the tournament or, in worse cases, of all future events.

Depending on your behaviour, organizers may report you to STK admins, and the consequences can be even bigger for you.
